1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Kuruvi Travel Companion App ("Kuruvi," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use the Kuruvi mobile application and related services (the "App").

This Policy forms part of, and is incorporated by reference into, our Terms & Conditions. By creating an account or using the App, you confirm that you have read and understood this Policy.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not create an account or use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide to us

When you register an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used to identify your account, sign you in, and send verification codes.
  • Username — your public display name, visible to other users.
  • Phone number — including the country code you select.
  • Date of birth — used solely to verify that you are at least 18 years old, as required by our Terms. We store a derived age-verification flag alongside it.
  • Password — if you register with email. Passwords are handled and stored by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us in readable form.

If you register using Google Sign-In, we receive your email address and basic Google profile information from Google instead of a password.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device type — whether you are using Android or iOS.
  • IP address and approximate country — recorded each time you sign in or sign out, as part of our account-security logs.
  • Authentication events — the time, sign-in method (email or Google), and outcome of each login and logout.
  • Failed sign-in attempts — counted so we can temporarily lock an account after repeated failures, to protect it against brute-force attacks.

2.3 Location information

With your permission, we collect your device's precise GPS location (latitude and longitude) at the moment you complete registration. This is used to show you travellers and trips near you.

We request location access only while you are using the App. You may refuse or later revoke this permission in your device settings; the App remains usable, though location-based matching will be unavailable or less accurate.

2.4 Content and activity

We store the content you create and the actions you take in the App, including:

  • Travel advertisements and trip details you post, including origin, destination, dates, and languages.
  • Travel groups you create or join, and group posts and discussions.
  • One-to-one and group chat messages, which are stored on our servers so they can be delivered and shown in your history.
  • Booking requests and connection requests you send or receive.
  • Reviews and ratings you give or receive, and your aggregate rating, rating count, and total trips.
  • Bookmarks you save.
  • Users you block, and reports you submit about other users or content.

Chat messages are not end-to-end encrypted. They are stored on our servers and can be accessed by us where necessary to investigate a report, comply with the law, or protect users.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, authenticate, and maintain your account.
  • To verify your email address using a one-time code sent to it.
  • To verify that you meet the minimum age requirement of 18 years.
  • To match you with travellers on similar routes and show relevant trips nearby.
  • To deliver your messages, booking requests, and connection requests.
  • To build reputation signals such as ratings and trip counts.
  • To send you push notifications about activity relevant to you, where you have allowed them.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, harassment, and unauthorised access.
  • To respond to your support enquiries.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the Services you signed up for, including account creation, matching, and messaging.
  • Consent — for precise location access and for push notifications. You may withdraw consent at any time through your device settings.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the platform safe and secure, prevent abuse, and improve the Services.
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to retain or disclose information by law.

5. How We Share Your Information

5.1 With other users

The following are visible to other users of the App: your username, your aggregate rating and trip count, any travel ads or group posts you create, and the content of messages you send to them. Your email address, phone number, date of birth, and precise location coordinates are not displayed on your public profile.

5.2 With service providers

We share information with the providers listed in Section 6, strictly so they can perform services on our behalf.

5.3 For legal and safety reasons

We may disclose information where we believe in good faith that it is necessary to comply with a law, regulation, or valid legal process; to enforce our Terms; or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Kuruvi, our users, or the public.

5.4 Business transfers

If Kuruvi is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a materially different privacy policy.

6. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third parties, each of which processes some of your data:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) — Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, and Cloud Messaging for push notifications. Hosts your account credentials, profile, content, and messages.
  • Google Sign-In (Google LLC) — if you choose to sign in with a Google account.
  • IP geolocation providers — when you sign in or out, your IP address is sent to icanhazip.com and api.iplocation.net to determine your approximate country for our security logs.
  • Our email provider — used to deliver one-time verification codes to your email address at sign-up.

These providers have their own privacy policies governing their use of data. We encourage you to review them.

7. Data Retention

  • Account and profile data — retained while your account is active.
  • Verification codes — short-lived and deleted shortly after they expire or are used.
  • Authentication and security logs — retained for a limited period for fraud prevention and account security.
  • Messages and posted content — retained while your account is active. Messages you have already sent may remain visible to their recipients.
  • Reports and safety records — may be retained after account deletion where needed to protect other users or meet legal obligations.

When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required or permitted to retain it.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your account and associated personal information.
  • Object to, or request that we restrict, certain processing.
  • Receive a copy of your information in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent for location access or push notifications at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

You can update your profile details and sign out from within the App. To exercise any of the rights above, contact us using the details in Section 13. We aim to respond within 30 days.

9. Security

Your account credentials are managed by Google Firebase Authentication. Data in transit is protected using industry-standard encryption, and access to stored data is restricted by server-side security rules. We temporarily lock accounts after repeated failed sign-in attempts.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for activity that occurs under your account.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is strictly for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete the account and its data promptly. If you believe a minor is using the App, please contact us.

11. International Data Transfers

Your information is stored and processed on Google Cloud infrastructure and may be transferred to, and processed in, countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified within the App or by email to the address associated with your account. The "Effective Date" above indicates when this version took effect. Your continued use of the App after a change takes effect constitutes acceptance.
