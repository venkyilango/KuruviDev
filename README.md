Kuruvi — Community Travel Companion
Kuruvi is a community-driven travel companion app built with Flutter that connects people traveling along the same routes. The platform is designed to make travel more collaborative, affordable, and socially connected, especially for users who prefer communicating in their native language.
The app enables travelers to:

Connect with people traveling on similar routes
Participate in route-based group chats
Use one-on-one messaging for direct coordination
Share travel updates, discussions, and local information
Coordinate low-cost package carrying between cities
Build trusted travel communities based on language and shared destinations
A key focus of Kuruvi is accessibility and ease of communication for elderly users and regional language speakers, helping them travel with greater confidence and social support.
Features
Real-time one-on-one chat
Route-based group discussions
Community-driven travel coordination
Language-friendly user experience
Package-sharing and cost optimization
Flutter-based cross-platform architecture
Tech Stack
Flutter
Real-time messaging architecture
Scalable mobile-first design
Modern UI/UX focused on accessibility
Vision
Kuruvi aims to create a trusted social travel ecosystem where people can collaborate, communicate, and support each other during their journeys while reducing travel and delivery costs through community participation.
